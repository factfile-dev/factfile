# Buy me a coffee

The factfile.md specification and tools are free and always will be. If they saved you time, a coffee helps keep the work going.

## Your coffee pays for

-   **Time on the specification**, reading every comment on the draft and working towards version 1.0.
-   **Better tools**, from the web generator to platform plugins that do not exist yet.
-   **Keeping it independent**: no ads, no tracking and no paid tier on this site.

[Buy me a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/alankiayi)

Payments are handled by the donation provider; this site never sees your card details.

## Other ways to help

-   [Star the project on GitHub](https://github.com/factfile-dev/factfile) and [comment on the draft](https://github.com/factfile-dev/factfile/issues).
-   Publish a factfile.md on your own website, then tell [@factfiledev](https://x.com/factfiledev) so it can be shared.
-   Tell a business owner or agent builder who would find it useful.
