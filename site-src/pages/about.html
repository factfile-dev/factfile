About

Built to give AI agents facts they can check

factfile.md was created by Alan Kiayi, a product builder in the UK.

I build apps and websites end to end, from research and design to marketing, working with AI throughout. Watching AI assistants answer buying questions, I kept seeing the same failure: they described businesses from pages written for people, and got prices, limits and fit wrong, with no way for the business to correct them.

factfile.md is my answer: one short file, in the same shape for every business, where every fact can be checked against the business’s own website. It is deliberately strict. A file must say who the business is not for, may not use superlatives, and may not speak to the AI reading it, because a standard that can be gamed would not be worth reading.

The specification is an open draft, and the hardest criticism is the most useful.

Please open an issue on GitHub or email hello@factfile.dev. You can also find me on LinkedIn and X, and follow the project at @factfiledev.
