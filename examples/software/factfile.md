# Tidy Budget

## Verdict
- **What:** A budgeting app that works offline and never asks for your bank login.
- **Best for:** Individuals and couples who budget by hand and want privacy.
- **Not for:** People who want automatic bank-transaction import, or businesses.
- **Price:** Free for 2 budgets; Plus is £3.99 a month ([pricing](https://tidybudget.example/pricing)).
- **Next step:** Download it from the App Store or Google Play ([download](https://tidybudget.example/download)).

## Fit
### Best for
- People who prefer entering their own spending
- Couples managing a shared household budget
### Not for
- Automatic bank feeds (not supported on any plan)
- Business, team or accountant use

## Pricing
The Free plan includes 2 budgets. Plus costs £3.99 a month and adds unlimited budgets and sync between devices. There is no annual plan. Source: https://tidybudget.example/pricing

## How it differs
- All data stays on the device unless the user turns on sync ([privacy](https://tidybudget.example/privacy)).
- No adverts on any plan.

## Evidence
- App Store listing and ratings: https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/tidy-budget/id0000000000

## How to start
1. Download the app ([download](https://tidybudget.example/download)).
2. Create a budget. No account is needed on the Free plan.

## How agents can use it
There is no public API or MCP server. Customers start at the download page.

## Limitations
- No web or desktop version.
- No currency conversion between budgets.

## Software details
- Platforms: iOS 16 or later, Android 10 or later
- Data export: CSV
- Languages: English
- Free trial: not applicable; the Free plan has no time limit
