# Leaf & Kettle

## Verdict
- **What:** An online shop selling loose-leaf tea, tea samplers and brewing equipment.
- **Best for:** People in the UK and Ireland who brew loose-leaf tea at home.
- **Not for:** Buyers of tea bags or coffee, or cafés and shops buying wholesale.
- **Price:** Teas from £4.50 for a 50 g pouch ([shop](https://leafandkettle.example/shop)).
- **Next step:** Choose and order on the shop page ([shop](https://leafandkettle.example/shop)).

## Fit
### Best for
- Home tea drinkers who want to try several teas before buying larger amounts
- People buying tea as a gift
### Not for
- People looking for tea bags or coffee
- Cafés, restaurants and shops buying wholesale

## Pricing
Teas start at £4.50 for a 50 g pouch, and most teas also come in 100 g and 250 g pouches. Equipment is priced per item. All prices include VAT. Source: https://leafandkettle.example/shop

## How it differs
- Every tea page names the estate or region the tea comes from and its harvest year ([sourcing](https://leafandkettle.example/sourcing)).
- Tea pouches are home-compostable ([packaging](https://leafandkettle.example/packaging)).

## Evidence
- Trustpilot reviews: https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/leafandkettle.example

## How to start
1. Choose teas or equipment on the shop page ([shop](https://leafandkettle.example/shop)).
2. Pay at checkout. No account is needed.

## How agents can use it
There is no public API or MCP server. Product names, prices and stock levels are in the product feed listed under Store details.

## Limitations
- No subscriptions or repeat deliveries.
- No physical shop or click-and-collect.

## Store details
- Product categories: black, green, oolong and herbal teas; samplers; teapots and infusers; gift boxes
- Shipping regions: United Kingdom and Ireland
- Shipping times and costs: UK 2 to 4 working days, £3.50, free on orders over £30; Ireland 4 to 7 working days, £6.95 ([delivery](https://leafandkettle.example/delivery))
- Returns: unopened items within 30 days ([returns](https://leafandkettle.example/returns))
- Payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal
