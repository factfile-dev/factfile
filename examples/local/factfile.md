# Hollybank Dog Grooming

## Verdict
- **What:** A dog grooming salon in south Birmingham that works by appointment only.
- **Best for:** Owners of dogs of any size who want a full groom or a bath and brush.
- **Not for:** Cats or other pets, or dogs that need sedation or veterinary care.
- **Price:** From £22 for a small-dog bath and brush; full grooms from £40 ([prices](https://hollybankgrooming.example/prices)).
- **Next step:** Book a time online ([book](https://hollybankgrooming.example/book)).

## Fit
### Best for
- Owners of small, medium and large dogs
- Puppies having their first groom, from 16 weeks old and after their vaccinations ([puppies](https://hollybankgrooming.example/puppies))
### Not for
- Owners of cats, rabbits or other animals
- Dogs that need sedation, or treatment for skin conditions

## Pricing
Prices depend on the dog's size and the service. A bath and brush for a small dog (up to 10 kg) costs £22. A full groom costs £40 for a small dog, £50 for a medium dog (10 to 25 kg) and £65 for a large dog (over 25 kg). Heavily matted coats can cost more; the extra is agreed before work starts. Source: https://hollybankgrooming.example/prices

## How it differs
- One dog is groomed at a time, so dogs do not wait in cages ([how we work](https://hollybankgrooming.example/how-we-work)).
- Owners can stay with their dog during its first visit.

## How to start
1. Choose a service and a time on the booking page ([book](https://hollybankgrooming.example/book)).
2. Enter the dog's breed, weight and any health needs.
3. Bring the dog at the booked time. A full groom takes 2 to 3 hours ([how we work](https://hollybankgrooming.example/how-we-work)).

## How agents can use it
There is no public API or MCP server. Bookings are made on the booking page or by phone.

## Limitations
- No collection or drop-off service.
- No mobile grooming at customers' homes.
